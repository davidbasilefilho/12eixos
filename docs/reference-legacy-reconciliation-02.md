## Ontology finding

The 1951 and 1989 declarations share the Socialist International umbrella, democratic methods, rights, and plural ownership. Different document editions do **not** independently prove that “social-democracy” and “democratic-socialism” are two distinct ideological families. Their revised vectors encode dated program excerpts; they are not evidence for an invented difference in doctrinal intensity. Both economic-ownership anchors are 60. The stronger 1951 planning anchor follows its explicit systematic-planning prescription, not its older date.

Accordingly, distinct-family selection of these two labels for the active 75 remains **provisional** pending separate referent/literature review by root. This batch preserves both catalog identities and does not duplicate them into the active taxonomy. It also does not validate all other families represented by international-party manifestos.
