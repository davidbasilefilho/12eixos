## Located sources and limits

**Germany.** Official [GG article 20](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/art_20.html), paragraphs 1–3, and [article 30](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gg/art_30.html) were read in full: explicit federal structure plus default Länder competence supports est80; constitutional exceptions remain. [Freedom House 2025](https://freedomhouse.org/country/germany/freedom-world/2025), Overview and Key Developments, supports representative democracy, parliamentary confidence, protections alongside protest restrictions, and the November documentary gender reform. These support rep80/pod40/mor60 with institutional and single-reform scope limits. The official English PDF/index was accessed, but later retrievals and article 140 failed; the index does not prove its incorporation clause. Consequently **rel is unknown**, even though the independently readable Weimar article 137 remains useful unmapped research. Generic equality alone did not establish mor.

**Denmark.** [University of Minnesota constitutional reproduction](https://hrlibrary.umn.edu/research/denmark-constitution.html): sections 15/29–31 parliamentary responsibility and elections; 71/77–79 liberty/expression/association; 75(2)/76 assistance and free elementary schooling; 4/6/67–70 established Lutheran church with religious freedom. This is a 1953 primary text in an edition labeled 1992, archived 2023; it is not represented as a comprehensive current consolidation, including the 2009 succession changes. [Freedom House 2025](https://freedomhouse.org/country/denmark/freedom-world/2025) Overview and Key Developments supply electoral practice and concrete deportation/privacy/coercion limits. eco60 concerns partial normative public provision, not ownership predominance. Established church with pluralism supports rel40, not the old secular direction. **mor remains unknown** because the abridged numeric table does not establish a reform.

**European trade.** [EUR-Lex consolidated 2016 TFUE](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:12016E/TXT), articles 28(1), 34, 206–207: internal opening, common external tariff, progressive reduction of external barriers and trade defense. Germany/Denmark com40 reflects regulated opening, not tariff-free universal trade or measured implementation. Greenland's territorial status is explicitly outside a blanket Danish EU inference.

**Uruguay.** Official IMPO individual constitutional pages were actually read: [77](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/constitucion/1967-1967/77) suffrage; [15](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/constitucion/1967-1967/15) detention safeguards; [71](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/constitucion/1967-1967/71) free official education; [230](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/constitucion/1967-1967/230) development planning with workers and public/private enterprises, 1996 revision; [5](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/constitucion/1967-1967/5) state religious separation. [Civil Code 83](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/codigo-civil/16603-1994/83), revised by Law 19075 on 2013-05-03, includes same-sex civil marriage. mor60 covers that reform alone. [Freedom House 2025](https://freedomhouse.org/country/uruguay/freedom-world/2025) narrative describes accepted peaceful elections and persistent press/court/prison difficulties; no rating arithmetic was transferred. Education does not establish public ownership dominance; planning is not comprehensive command allocation. Article 50's import-substitution protection was read but does not establish implemented tariff policy: **com remains unknown**.

**New Zealand.** [Governor-General constitutional description](https://gg.govt.nz/office-governor-general/roles-and-functions-governor-general/constitutional-role/constitution), Constitution Act 1986 and political-party sections, and [Justice ICCPR page](https://www.justice.govt.nz/justice-sector-policy/constitutional-issues-and-human-rights/human-rights/international-human-rights/international-covenant-on-civil-and-political-rights/) updated 2024-04-24 supply parliamentary institutions, reservations and the 2023 prisoner-suffrage criticism. Official legislation-host retrieval failed; these are honestly labeled institutional declarations rather than read statute text. [MFAT agreement chapter 2](https://www.mfat.govt.nz/assets/Trade-agreements/EU-NZ-FTA/Chapters/2.-National-Treatment-and-Market-Access-for-Goods.pdf), articles 2.1/2.5/2.11, was read: reciprocal liberalization with schedules/exceptions, effective 2024-05-01. [Government marriage guide](https://www.govt.nz/browse/family-and-whanau/getting-married/), Civil unions section, recognizes relationships regardless of gender; mor60 is partial and based on the undated current declaration. [Freedom House 2025](https://freedomhouse.org/country/new-zealand/freedom-world/2025) Overview supplies democracy/liberties and minority discrimination; its literal Key Developments heading says 2025 despite chronology referencing the 2023 coalition. No silent date correction or numeric-table inference was made. **est, imi and tec remain unknown**: parliamentary sovereignty, one language policy, or a digital plan do not independently cover those axes.
