## Actual primary readings and scope

[IWA official statutes](https://www.iwa-ait.org/content/statutes), approved 9–10 December 2022 and webpage updated 10 February 2023: introduction and principles I–II plus organization VI actually read. II.2–4 establishes free producer councils, abolition of State functions, whole production/distribution on an agreed plan, and a broad bottom-up federal social order. This supports territorial/social authority, productive property and economic coordination. II.7 and II.10 oppose militarism and standing armies while allowing worker militias, defensive force and assistance to defend revolution. Hence diplomacy is a moderate anti-war anchor with the exceptions retained. Broad general franchise, legal opposition and judicial safeguards are not established by union congress rules: representation and power remain unknown pending separate breadth review.

[Bonanno's primary translation](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/alfredo-m-bonanno-insurrectionalist-anarchism), metadata Italian 1999 / English 2009, first introduction dated 21 November 1998 in the actual body. First introduction web 120–178 and 190–208 actually read: opposition to State/property, autonomous affinity organization, mixed anarchist/non-anarchist base nuclei and their termination when their goal ends. It rejects a lasting congress-governed programme organization. This is normatively political collective authority and action, although the author frames it as a project/method rather than a complete future constitution. That qualification does not create twelve axis positions. No operational instructions are reproduced in the profile.

[Hahnel's author-hosted manuscript](https://www.sscc.wisc.edu/soc/faculty/pages/wright/Published%20writing/Alternatives%20to%20Capitalism.pdf), chapter 1 printed pp2–8 / PDF zero-based 12–19, actually read. It explicitly defends affected-interest decision power, worker/consumer councils, balanced jobs, peer-assessed effort and iterative planning without markets or central planners. Chapter 5 note55, printed p96 / PDF107, explicitly classifies this model as social ownership and worker self-management, rather than private ownership or external direction. This note is Hahnel's own author section, not Wright's or the editorial introduction. It supports general productive property; the annual whole-system production/consumption procedure supports economic coordination. It does not establish all State or civil authority axes.

Hahnel expressly says the economic model is **not a transition strategy or political programme** (chapter 1, web134–135). This limitation is preserved. The affirmative justification of collective economic institutions still meets the parent's political-economic inclusion criterion; unlike Veblen's conditional design, the author endorses their justice and legitimacy. Majority consent before eventual market replacement and present cooperation to reform markets are counterevidence against an invented immediate compulsory transition. Claims of feasibility or efficiency remain author arguments, not observed results validated by this review.
