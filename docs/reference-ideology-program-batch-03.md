All evidence confidence is medium. Strong or moderate direction describes the source-based editorial position, independently of confidence. Numbers are versioned ordinal anchors, not measurements or questionnaire answers. Every claim uses declaration basis; no stated objective becomes an observed outcome.

DSA representation is moderate because multiparty representation and expanded voting coexist with executive/judiciary subordination to Congress. Migration admission and anti-racism alone do not establish the full cultural-pluralism axis. Police/prison abolition and adult intimate autonomy form the proposed broader liberty case; drug-health policy is not a claim that all drug production/sale is legalized. Foreign-war opposition retains resistance to occupation. Noninterference retains international prosecution as counterevidence. Public ownership does not claim every small business or personal asset is socialized. Economic regulation uses labour, housing prices and fiscal rules across several functions rather than inferring planning from ownership alone. Broad bodily/family/gender freedoms support the proposed moral direction, while missing abortion-stage, euthanasia and parental details remain explicit.

SPD territorial decentralization is moderate because exact constitutional competences and unlimited local veto are not established. Political opposition, minority rights and separated branches support a broader democratic case. Civil liberty combines rights against the State, independent media/judges and uncensored art; it does not imply every digital-surveillance or drug-policy rule. Anti-war norms retain national defence and international coercive order. The private-property direction is only moderate: public competitors, natural-monopoly provision, conditional socialization and decentralized public management are explicit counters. Macro planning is indirect and preserves market choice, collective bargaining and central-bank autonomy. World-trade openness retains agricultural market/price controls and does not mean zero tariffs. Religion, contemporary moral controversies and comprehensive technology adoption remain unknown.

The EST claim concerns the distribution of public authority across the explicitly named Bund, Länder and Gemeinden (p5, 141–148), rather than administrative funding alone. The following municipal self-government and financial-support clauses complement that constitutional-order norm. Detailed legislative competences, unconditional local veto and secession are unestablished, so the proposed anchor remains moderate60.

Later independent actual source/construct review recommends all draft DSA7/SPD8 axes with these counters.
