This is an index-level screening of all 75 and an **initial bounded documentary review of five referents, extended below to seven**, not verification of 75 independent doctrines. No catalog vectors or sources were modified here.

## Definition and distinctness rule

The working inclusion object is a normative political doctrine or tradition with an identifiable account of legitimate authority or collective social/economic order and action. A policy instrument, technical project, regime label or evaluative framework by itself does not meet that definition. A state may author a doctrine: its origin is not a disqualifier. The number of documented axes is unrelated to conceptual distinctness.

An umbrella and subtradition can be provisionally retained together if their additional commitments are located and nesting is disclosed. A different author, nationality, year or name is not evidence of distinctness. Full pairwise checks remain incomplete; each selected record retains an explicit contrast status.

## Actually inspected primary referents

**Lorenzo Kom’boa Ervin.** [Anarchism and the Black Revolution](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/lorenzo-kom-boa-ervin-anarchism-and-the-black-revolution), dedication dated September 1993, Chapter 1 “Race and Class” and “Defeat white supremacy”, Chapter 2 commune headings and Chapter 3 organizational discussion. Racial domination cannot simply be postponed to a class revolution. The page is the **1993 edition**, although the current catalog period says 1979–1983; this source discrepancy is reported without changing the profile under this ownership. One author's intervention does not define all Black anarchisms.

**G. D. H. Cole.** [Guild Socialism Re-Stated scanned book](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/57/Guild_socialism_re-stated_(IA_guildsocialismre00coleiala).pdf), 232 PDF pages, 1920 first publication/1921 reprint. Preface explicitly denies a strict guild orthodoxy. Chapter I, printed pages 9–14/PDF pages 13–18, extends industrial control to political reconstruction and active self-government throughout society. The contents identify producer, consumer and communal institutions. Further chapters must be examined before claiming an independently verified contrast with all forms of democratic/market socialism. Earlier Marxists/Archive/Routledge opens failed; the actual scan was retrieved through Wikimedia.

**Samuel Edward Konkin III.** [New Libertarian Manifesto](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/samuel-edward-konkin-iii-new-libertarian-manifesto), first 1980/second 1983 prefaces with separate 2006 publisher foreword. Chapters I–III were inspected, including explicit rejection of “partyarchy”, voluntary market institutions and countereconomics. This supports a transition doctrine beyond a shared market-anarchist endpoint. Contrast with [LP platform section 3.6](https://lp.org/platform-page/) is direct: LP proposes electoral reform. It does not prove agorism and anarcocapitalism are mutually exclusive philosophical families. The originally supplied agorism.info PDF failed; no URL failure is promoted to doctrine failure.

**Emma Goldman and Mujeres Libres.** [Goldman's 1906 essay](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/emma-goldman-the-tragedy-of-woman-s-emancipation), paragraphs on suffrage, home/factory dependence, marriage and internal social constraints, supports a normative gender-emancipation critique but alone does not establish the whole Spanish movement. The actual [Nash documentary collection](https://mirror.anarhija.net/es.theanarchistlibrary.org/mirror/m/mn/mary-nash-mujeres-libres.a4.pdf), 1975 edition, printed pages 61–64, reproduces “Cómo organizar una agrupación Mujeres Libres”: coordinating committees without command, literacy, labor training and collective social assistance. Its 96-page imposed PDF has non-sequential printed-page order. We do **not** claim to have read “Finalidades”; follow-up retrieval failed. The FAL source also failed. Full contrast with Combahee/feminist socialism is still pending.

**Kokutai.** [Columbia's 1937 excerpts](https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/ps/japan/kokutai.pdf), five PDF pages, “Introduction”, “Our Mission” and longer selection, show an explicit normative national polity and imperial historical unity. The text criticizes Western individualist foundations and addresses other doctrines. This is evidence of a state-authored doctrine; it is not merely a governmental time slice. Therefore it remains selected under the narrower label **Doutrina imperial do kokutai, 1937**. This is a selection label, not a change to the underlying catalog ID/name. The translation is an excerpt from the 2005 anthology, not all 156 original pages. Systematic contrast with fascism/absolute monarchy remains provisional.

## Accepted bounded contrast group: ecological transition

The exact [Paris 2008 declaration](https://isecoeco.org/pdf/Newsletter_2008_Oct.pdf) was recovered as a primary-text reproduction in ISEE's October 2008 newsletter, printed/PDF pages 21–22, under “DECLARATION”. The immediately preceding Tilburg declaration is a different document and is not used for these claims. Paris calls for reducing excess national/global ecological footprints, transformation of economic systems, eventual steady-state consumption and equity/participatory democracy; it explicitly distinguishes voluntary degrowth from uncontrolled contraction. This establishes a political normative referent, beyond an economic indicator. The catalog's original PDF and the movement's Drive attachment failed retrieval; those failures are logged rather than concealed.

The [2015 Ecomodernist Manifesto](https://www.ecomodernism.org/manifesto-english), opening and sections 1–2, 6–7, advocates absolute decoupling that allows economic growth while aggregate impacts fall, active public innovation and civil/private institutions, plus democracy and pluralism. It explicitly rejects reducing modernization to laissez-faire. Thus the primary texts differ in their desired relationship between economic expansion and ecological transition. This is a **bounded two-sided documentary contrast**, not a judgment that either program's scientific predictions are correct. Neighboring Green and ecosocialist traditions, internal differences and later versions remain unreviewed. Both selected rows can now carry `bounded-primary-neighbor-contrast` while the full 75 certification stays false.

## Additional primary check: Combahee overlap

The Duke URL failed, but the actual [1977 statement in Yale's reader](https://americanstudies.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/Keyword%20Coalition_Readings.pdf), PDF pages 1–6, especially section 2 on pages 3–5, was read. It explicitly joins racial, gender, class and sexuality oppression, collective work/distribution and an extension of Marxist class analysis. Those commitments overlap with the Black-anarchist and anarchafeminist referents. Combahee's lack of an anarchist label does **not** establish a positive state-centered program. Consequently the authority contrast remains unresolved after actual two-sided reading; no convenient opposition is invented.
